## 1. Purpose and Scope

This procedure ranks pressure vessels and piping circuits by risk, and sets
how that ranking changes inspection priority and intervals. Default intervals
are set by SOP-INS-014 Clause 2 for vessels and SOP-INS-017 Clauses 3 and 6
for piping.

## 2. Risk Ranking

### 2.1 Probability of Failure (PoF)

PoF is scored from the remaining life of the governing location:
PoF 5 where remaining life is under 2 years or any reading is below t-min;
PoF 4 where it is 2 to under 4 years; PoF 3 where it is 4 to under 10 years;
PoF 2 where it is 10 to 20 years; PoF 1 where it is over 20 years.

### 2.2 Consequence of Failure (CoF)

CoF 5: lethal or toxic service (SOP-INS-014 Clause 2.3), or flammable service
above 20 bar(g). CoF 4: flammable service at or below 20 bar(g). CoF 3: steam
and hot condensate service, or non-flammable service above 20 bar(g).
CoF 2: non-flammable, non-toxic service at or below 20 bar(g). CoF 1: utility
service such as cooling water or instrument air.

### 2.3 Risk Score

The risk score is PoF multiplied by CoF, from 1 to 25. Example: remaining life
6.2 years gives PoF 3; flammable service at 10.5 bar(g) gives CoF 4; the risk
score is 3 × 4 = 12.

## 3. Use of the Ranking

### 3.1 High Risk

Equipment with a risk score of 15 or more is High risk. Its inspection
intervals are one half of those set by SOP-INS-014 Clause 2 or SOP-INS-017
Clause 6, and it is inspected first in any shutdown.

### 3.2 Medium Risk

Equipment with a risk score from 8 to 14 is Medium risk. Its inspection
intervals are those set by SOP-INS-014 Clause 2 or SOP-INS-017 Clause 6,
unchanged.

### 3.3 Low Risk

Equipment with a risk score of 7 or less is Low risk. Its intervals are
unchanged. A Low ranking may support a proposal to extend an interval, but the
extension is decided under SOP-INS-014 Clause 2.5 for a vessel, or approved
under SOP-OPS-008 Clause 2.7 for piping.

## 4. Reassessment

The risk ranking of an item shall be reassessed after every inspection of it,
after any change to it processed under SOP-ENG-009, and in any case at least
every 36 months.

## 5. Limits of Risk-Based Inspection

### 5.1 No Extension by Ranking Alone

A risk ranking under this procedure does not by itself authorise the extension
of any inspection interval. A pressure vessel interval may only be extended
under SOP-INS-014 Clause 2.5, which requires a documented Fitness-For-Service
assessment approved by the Head of Inspection.

### 5.2 Approval of the Plan

The risk-based inspection plan, and every change to an item's risk score, is
approved by the Head of Inspection before it is used to schedule work.
