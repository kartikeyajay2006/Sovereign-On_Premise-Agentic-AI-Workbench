# INV-2015-014 — Incident Investigation Summary: Loss of Containment on Crude Overhead Line P-2104-OVHD-01

## 1. Summary of the Incident

At 02:40 on 14 November 2015 a pinhole leak opened on the 8-inch crude column
overhead line P-2104-OVHD-01, at the first elbow downstream of the wash-water
injection point. About 140 kg of hydrocarbon vapour and condensate was
released before the unit was shut down. There was no fire and no injury.

## 2. Classification

The release of 100 kg or more of hydrocarbon made this a Major incident under
SOP-HSE-004 Clause 3.1. The investigation was led by the Plant Manager with
the HSE Manager, as SOP-HSE-004 Clause 4.1 requires.

## 3. Direct Cause

Localised corrosion in the wash-water mixing zone thinned the elbow wall until
it perforated. The elbow had no condition monitoring location; the nearest
thickness point was 6 m upstream and had shown normal readings.

## 4. Root Causes

4.1 The inspection programme did not treat injection points as circuits of
their own, so no CML was placed where mixing corrosion concentrates.

4.2 The injection quill had been modified in 2012 without a Management of
Change, and the new orientation gave poor mixing.

## 5. Corrective Actions

5.1 Replace P-2104-OVHD-01 from the column nozzle to the condenser inlet
header. Completed 12 March 2016.

5.2 Treat every injection point circuit as Class 1, with a CML on the first
downstream fitting. Implemented as SOP-INS-017 Clauses 3.1 and 3.4.

5.3 Redesign the injection quill under SOP-ENG-009, raised as MOC-2016-003.

## 6. Status

All corrective actions were closed on 30 June 2016, and this summary was
approved by the Plant Manager. The full investigation file is held by the HSE
Manager.
