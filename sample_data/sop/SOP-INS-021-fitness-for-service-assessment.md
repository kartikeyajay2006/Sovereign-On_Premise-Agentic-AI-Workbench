# SOP-INS-021 — Fitness-For-Service Assessment of Pressure Equipment

**Document:** SOP-INS-021
**Revision:** 3.0
**Department:** Inspection & Integrity
**Effective date:** 01 September 2025
**Classification:** Confidential — Internal Use Only
**Related:** SOP-INS-014 (Inspection), SOP-MNT-022 (CUI)

---

## 1. Purpose

To define when a Fitness-For-Service (FFS) assessment is required, which level
of assessment applies, and what must be recorded before equipment showing
degradation is returned to service.

## 2. When an Assessment Is Required

An FFS assessment shall be raised when any of the following is true:

- Measured wall thickness is below t-min but no through-wall defect exists
- Remaining life calculated per SOP-INS-014 Clause 4 is less than 2 years
- Local metal loss exceeds 20% of nominal wall thickness
- A crack-like flaw of any length is detected in a pressure boundary
- Equipment is proposed for continued service beyond its registered design life

## 3. Assessment Levels

| Level | When it applies | Who may perform it | Typical duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | Uniform metal loss, geometry within code limits, no crack-like flaws | Inspection Engineer | 1–2 days |
| Level 2 | Local metal loss, pitting, or blisters requiring detailed stress analysis | Senior Integrity Engineer | 1–2 weeks |
| Level 3 | Crack-like flaws, creep damage, or numerical stress analysis required | Approved third-party specialist under Head of Inspection | 3–6 weeks |

A Level 1 assessment that fails shall be escalated to Level 2 rather than
being used as a basis for rejection.

## 4. Remaining Strength Factor

4.1 The Remaining Strength Factor (RSF) shall be calculated for all local
metal loss assessments.

4.2 The acceptance criterion is **RSF ≥ 0.90**. Equipment with an RSF below
0.90 shall not return to service without a re-rating or a repair.

4.3 Where RSF falls between 0.90 and 0.95, the inspection interval shall be
halved and the location placed on the accelerated monitoring register.

## 5. Re-Rating

5.1 Where the assessment shows the equipment cannot sustain its registered
design pressure, a re-rated Maximum Allowable Working Pressure (MAWP) may be
established:

    MAWP_rerated = MAWP_original × RSF

5.2 A re-rating requires the written approval of the Head of Inspection and
the Plant Manager, and the equipment nameplate shall be updated within 30 days.

5.3 Re-rated equipment shall be re-assessed at every subsequent inspection,
without exception.

## 6. Interim Operation

6.1 Equipment awaiting assessment may continue to operate only where all of
the following hold:

- No through-wall defect and no active leak
- Measured thickness is at or above t-min at every location
- Operating pressure is reduced to 90% of the registered MAWP or lower
- The equipment is inspected at not more than 30-day intervals
- The interim arrangement is recorded in an approval note and countersigned

6.2 Interim operation shall not exceed 180 days from the date of the finding.

## 7. Records

Every assessment shall record the calculation inputs, the method applied, the
RSF obtained, the disposition, and the identity of the assessor and approver.
Assessment records are retained for the operating life of the equipment plus
ten years and shall not leave the organisation's controlled network.
