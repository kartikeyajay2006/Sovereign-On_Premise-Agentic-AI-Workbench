## 1. Purpose and Scope

This procedure sets how equipment is isolated from every source of hazardous
energy — electrical, mechanical, hydraulic, pneumatic, process pressure and
fluid, and thermal — and locked and tagged before anyone works on it.

## 2. Isolation Methods

### 2.1 Positive Isolation

Positive isolation is a physical break: a spectacle blind or spade at a
flange, or a removed spool with blanks fitted to both open ends. It is
required for confined space entry (SOP-OPS-012 Clause 3.1) and for hot work on
equipment that has contained hydrocarbons (SOP-OPS-011 Clause 5.1).

### 2.2 Double Block and Bleed

Double block and bleed is two closed, locked valves with an open, vented bleed
between them. It may be used for work other than confined space entry and hot
work on equipment that has contained hydrocarbons.

### 2.3 Single Valve Isolation

A single closed and locked valve is permitted only in non-hydrocarbon utility
service below 10 bar(g) and 60 °C. It is never permitted in hydrocarbon,
toxic or steam service.

## 3. Locks and Tags

### 3.1 Personal Locks

Every person working under an isolation shall fit their own personal lock to
the lock box that holds the isolation keys. The isolation shall not be removed
until every personal lock has been removed by its owner.

### 3.2 Tags

Each isolation point shall carry a tag stating the isolation certificate
number, the name of the Isolation Authority and the date the isolation was
applied.

## 4. Verification of Isolation

Before work starts, the Isolation Authority and the Permit Receiver shall
verify the isolation together: by a start attempt (try-out) for electrical
isolation, and by confirming zero pressure at the bleed point for process
isolation.

## 5. Removal of Locks

### 5.1 Removal by the Owner

A personal lock may be removed only by the person who fitted it, once their
work is complete and they have left the equipment.

### 5.2 Removal of an Absent Person's Lock

Where the owner of a lock has left site and cannot be contacted, the lock may
be removed only with the written authority of the Shift Superintendent, after
confirming that the person is off site and the equipment is safe. The removal
shall be reported under SOP-HSE-004 Clause 2.

## 6. Relief Device Isolation Valves

Isolating a relief device while its equipment is in service needs an isolation
certificate under this procedure and the conditions of SOP-INS-025 Clause 5.

## 7. Records

Isolation certificates are retained for 3 years, with the list of personal
locks fitted and removed and the times of each.
