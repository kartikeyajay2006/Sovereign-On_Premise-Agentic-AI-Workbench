## 1. Status and Distribution

Draft Rev 0.3 for internal review. This memorandum is not released for
construction and contains unreleased design information. Distribution is
limited to staff cleared for Restricted information, in any department.

## 2. Basis for Replacement

Inspection report INS-2026-0417 recorded corrosion under insulation on shell
course 2 of V-2104, with a minimum reading of 9.4 mm against 12.0 mm nominal
and cladding damage over about 35% of that course. Replacing the vessel at the
2027 turnaround is proposed instead of repeated CUI repair.

## 3. Design Conditions of the Replacement Vessel

Replacement vessel tag V-2104R. Design pressure 12.0 bar(g). Design
temperature 160 °C. MAWP 12.0 bar(g). Inside diameter 1200 mm. Material
SA-516 Gr.70. Corrosion allowance 3.0 mm. Nominal shell thickness 14.0 mm.
Joint efficiency 1.0 (full radiography).

## 4. Protection Against Corrosion Under Insulation

Thermal spray aluminium coating on the shell and heads, closed-cell
water-repellent insulation, and sealed cladding penetrations, following items
2 and 3 of the mitigation hierarchy in SOP-MNT-022 Clause 5.

## 5. Relief Protection

PSV-2104A is to be reset from 10.5 bar(g) to 12.0 bar(g) to match the new
MAWP, within the limit of SOP-INS-025 Clause 4.1. The change of set pressure
is a change under SOP-ENG-009 Clause 2.1.

## 6. Cost and Schedule

Estimated installed cost: ₹42 lakh, including removal of the existing vessel.
Planned installation: 2027 turnaround. The replacement is a Major change under
SOP-ENG-009 Clause 3.1, and MOC-2026-014 has been raised. Expenditure approval
will be sought under SOP-OPS-008.

## 7. Open Items

Nozzle loads from the revised overhead piping and the foundation check are not
yet confirmed. Any value in this memorandum may change before release.
