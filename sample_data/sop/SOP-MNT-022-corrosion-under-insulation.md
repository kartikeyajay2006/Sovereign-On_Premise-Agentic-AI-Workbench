# SOP-MNT-022 — Management of Corrosion Under Insulation (CUI)

**Document:** SOP-MNT-022
**Revision:** 2.1
**Department:** Inspection & Integrity
**Effective date:** 15 June 2025
**Classification:** Confidential — Internal Use Only

---

## 1. Purpose

To define the identification, assessment and mitigation of corrosion under
insulation on insulated piping and equipment operating within the CUI
susceptibility temperature range.

## 2. Susceptibility Range

2.1 Carbon steel equipment operating between **-4 °C and 175 °C** shall be
treated as susceptible to CUI.

2.2 Equipment subject to cyclic operation, intermittent service, or steam
tracing shall be treated as susceptible regardless of normal operating
temperature.

2.3 Austenitic stainless steel operating between 50 °C and 175 °C shall be
assessed for external chloride stress corrosion cracking in addition to CUI.

## 3. Inspection Triggers

An insulation removal inspection shall be raised when any of the following
is observed:

- Damaged, missing or displaced cladding
- Staining, rust bleeding or discolouration at cladding joints
- Water ingress at penetrations, supports, nozzles or valve boxes
- Insulation that is soft, compressed or waterlogged to the touch
- Equipment returning from a shutdown where insulation was disturbed

## 4. Assessment

4.1 Where cladding damage exceeds 20% of the surface area of an insulated
section, that section shall be classified as a **Medium** severity finding
under SOP-INS-014 Clause 5.

4.2 Localised pitting discovered beneath insulation shall be measured by pit
gauge and reported with maximum pit depth and pit density per 100 cm².

4.3 Where remaining wall thickness beneath a CUI site falls below t-min as
defined in SOP-INS-014 Clause 3, the finding shall be escalated to **High**
severity and the equipment withdrawn from service within 24 hours.

## 5. Mitigation Hierarchy

Mitigation shall be selected in the following order of preference:

1. Remove insulation permanently where thermal requirement no longer exists
2. Replace with closed-cell, water-repellent insulation systems
3. Apply a qualified thermal spray aluminium or CUI-resistant coating system
4. Restore cladding integrity and seal all penetrations
5. Apply accelerated monitoring where none of the above is practicable

## 6. Reporting

6.1 CUI findings shall be recorded against the parent equipment tag, not
against the insulation system.

6.2 An approval note prepared under SOP-INS-014 Clause 6 shall reference this
procedure where a CUI mechanism is implicated in the finding.

6.3 Photographic evidence of the exposed surface is mandatory for every
insulation removal inspection.
