## 1. Purpose and Scope

This procedure governs the inspection, bench testing and setting of pressure
safety valves (PSVs) and rupture discs that protect pressure vessels and
piping within the refinery battery limits.

## 2. Test Intervals

### 2.1 Test Interval in Clean Service

A PSV in clean, non-fouling, non-corrosive service shall be removed and bench
tested at intervals not exceeding 48 months.

### 2.2 Test Interval in Fouling or Corrosive Service

A PSV in fouling or corrosive service shall be removed and bench tested at
intervals not exceeding 24 months.

### 2.3 Tests Outside the Interval

A PSV shall also be bench tested before return to service after any known lift
in service, and whenever the equipment it protects is re-rated under
SOP-INS-021 Clause 5.

## 3. As-Received Test

### 3.1 Test Before Any Work

Every PSV removed for testing shall first be tested as received, before any
cleaning or adjustment, and its opening pressure recorded.

### 3.2 Failure of the As-Received Test

The as-received test fails when the valve opens at a pressure more than 10%
above its set pressure, or does not open at all. For a set pressure of
10.5 bar(g) the valve fails if it opens above 11.55 bar(g). A failed test is a
High severity finding on the protected equipment (Clause 3.3).

### 3.3 Severity of a Failed As-Received Test

A failed as-received test is a failed relief device: a High severity finding
against the protected vessel or piping under SOP-INS-014 Clause 5.1. The
protected equipment is withdrawn from service within 24 hours, and the
approval authority is the Head of Inspection + Plant Manager. The failure is
also reported as a near miss within 24 hours under SOP-HSE-004 Clause 2.3.

### 3.4 Setting After Overhaul

After overhaul a PSV shall be set to open within ±3% of its set pressure, or
within ±0.14 bar where the set pressure is 4.8 bar(g) or less.

## 4. Set Pressure

### 4.1 Set Pressure Limit

The set pressure of a PSV shall not exceed the MAWP of the equipment it
protects, including any re-rated MAWP established under SOP-INS-021 Clause 5.

### 4.2 Re-Rated Equipment

Where equipment is re-rated, its PSVs shall be reset to the re-rated MAWP or
lower before the equipment returns to service. A change of set pressure is a
change under SOP-ENG-009.

## 5. Isolation Valves and Inlet Lines

5.1 Block valves under a PSV shall be locked open while the protected
equipment is in service. Closing one requires an isolation certificate under
SOP-OPS-015 and a second tested relief path on the equipment.

5.2 The inlet pressure loss to a PSV shall not exceed 3% of its set pressure.

## 6. Records

The as-received result, work done, final set pressure, test date and next due
date shall be recorded against the PSV tag and the protected equipment tag.
Records are retained for the life of the equipment plus seven years.
