## 1. Purpose and Scope

This procedure controls every change to process chemistry, technology,
equipment design conditions, materials, operating limits, relief systems or
operating procedures, so that its hazards are reviewed and approved before it
is made. A replacement in kind is not a change.

## 2. What Counts as a Change

### 2.1 Changes That Require a Management of Change

The following require a Management of Change (MOC): re-rating of pressure
equipment (SOP-INS-021 Clause 5.4); a change of relief device set pressure
(SOP-INS-025 Clause 4.2); a change of material or design conditions; a new
or modified injection point; hot tapping (SOP-OPS-011 Clause 5.2); and any
temporary repair such as a clamp or a leak box.

### 2.2 Replacement in Kind

A replacement that meets the original design specification in every respect
is not a change and needs no MOC. The Process Engineer decides whether a
proposal is a replacement in kind and records that decision with its reasons.

## 3. Classes of Change

### 3.1 Major Change

A Major change alters design conditions or relief systems, or introduces a
hazard not present before. It needs a team hazard review led by the Process
Engineer, and is approved by the Plant Manager. Approving a change does not
approve its expenditure, which follows SOP-OPS-008 Clauses 2.9 to 2.11.

### 3.2 Minor Change

A Minor change is any change that is not Major. It needs a checklist hazard
review by the Process Engineer, and is approved by the head of the department
that owns the equipment.

### 3.3 Temporary Change

A temporary change shall state an expiry date not more than 90 days after it
is implemented. Before that date it shall be removed, or approved again as a
permanent change under this procedure.

## 4. Before Implementation

4.1 No change shall be implemented before its MOC is approved.

4.2 A pre-startup safety review (PSSR) shall be completed and signed before
equipment affected by a Major change is returned to service.

4.3 Operating procedures and drawings shall be updated, and the people
affected trained, before the changed equipment starts up.

## 5. Emergency Changes

A change needed at once to prevent harm may be made on the verbal approval of
the Shift Superintendent. The MOC shall be raised within 24 hours and approved
or reversed within 7 days. The event shall be reported under SOP-HSE-004
Clause 2.2.

## 6. Records

Every MOC, with its hazard review, approvals, PSSR and closure, is retained for
the life of the plant. The risk ranking of any item changed is reassessed
under SOP-INS-030 Clause 4.
