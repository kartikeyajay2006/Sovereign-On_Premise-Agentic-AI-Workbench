# SOP-OPS-008 — Approval Authority and Delegation Matrix

**Document:** SOP-OPS-008
**Revision:** 5.1
**Department:** Operations
**Effective date:** 15 July 2025
**Classification:** Confidential — Internal Use Only

---

## 1. Purpose

To define who may approve what, so that every decision affecting plant
integrity, expenditure or safety carries an identifiable and competent
approver.

## 2. Authority Matrix

| Decision | Inspection Engineer | Head of Inspection | Plant Manager | Director |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accept a Low severity finding | Approve | — | — | — |
| Accept a Medium severity finding | Recommend | Approve | — | — |
| Accept a High severity finding | Recommend | Recommend | Approve | — |
| Withdraw equipment from service | Initiate | Approve | Notify | — |
| Return equipment to service after repair | Recommend | Approve | — | — |
| Re-rate equipment (reduce MAWP) | Recommend | Approve | Approve | Notify |
| Extend an inspection interval | Recommend | Approve | — | — |
| Interim operation under SOP-INS-021 Clause 6 | Recommend | Approve | Approve | — |
| Expenditure up to ₹5 lakh | — | Approve | — | — |
| Expenditure ₹5–50 lakh | — | Recommend | Approve | — |
| Expenditure above ₹50 lakh | — | Recommend | Recommend | Approve |

## 3. Rules of Application

3.1 Where a decision falls under more than one row, the **highest** authority
named applies.

3.2 An approver shall not approve a recommendation they themselves authored.

3.3 Approval is personal and may not be delegated by email. Delegation during
absence requires a written delegation notice naming the delegate, the scope,
and the period, countersigned by the delegating authority.

3.4 An approval given without the evidence required by the governing procedure
is void.

## 4. Approval Note Content

Every approval note shall carry, as a minimum:

- The decision being sought, stated in one sentence
- The equipment or subject affected, by tag number
- The findings relied upon, with their severity classification
- The calculations relied upon, with inputs shown
- The clause of the governing procedure authorising the decision
- The compensating measures, where the decision permits continued operation
- The name and role of the recommending officer and the approving authority

## 5. Records

Approval notes are retained for the operating life of the equipment plus seven
years. An approval note that permits continued operation of degraded equipment
shall be reviewed at every subsequent inspection of that equipment.
