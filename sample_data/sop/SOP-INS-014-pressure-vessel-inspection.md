# SOP-INS-014 — Pressure Vessel External and Internal Inspection

**Document:** SOP-INS-014
**Revision:** 4.2
**Department:** Inspection & Integrity
**Effective date:** 01 April 2025
**Classification:** Confidential — Internal Use Only
**Supersedes:** SOP-INS-014 Rev 3.8

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This procedure governs the periodic external and internal inspection of
pressure vessels operating within the refinery battery limits, including
separators, knock-out drums, surge vessels and accumulators registered under
the Static and Mobile Pressure Vessels (Unfired) Rules.

This procedure applies to all vessels with a design pressure exceeding
1.0 bar(g) and an internal volume exceeding 500 litres.

## 2. Inspection Intervals

| Vessel service | External inspection | Internal inspection | Thickness survey |
|---|---|---|---|
| Non-corrosive, non-lethal | 24 months | 72 months | 48 months |
| Corrosive service | 12 months | 48 months | 24 months |
| Lethal / toxic service | 12 months | 36 months | 24 months |
| Steam and hot condensate | 18 months | 60 months | 36 months |

Intervals shall not be extended beyond the values in Clause 2 without a
documented Fitness-For-Service assessment approved by the Head of Inspection.

## 3. Minimum Allowable Thickness

3.1 The minimum allowable thickness (t-min) for any shell or head course
shall be calculated per ASME Section VIII Division 1, UG-27, using the
corroded condition and the vessel's registered design pressure.

3.2 Where the calculated t-min is less than 6.0 mm for carbon steel shell
courses, the value of 6.0 mm shall be adopted as the structural minimum
irrespective of the pressure calculation.

3.3 A vessel shall be withdrawn from service immediately when any measured
thickness reading falls below t-min, or when the projected thickness at the
next scheduled inspection falls below t-min.

## 4. Corrosion Rate and Remaining Life

4.1 The short-term corrosion rate shall be calculated as:

    corrosion_rate (mm/year) = (t_previous - t_current) / years_between_inspections

4.2 The long-term corrosion rate shall be calculated from the original
nominal thickness and the installation date.

4.3 The governing corrosion rate is the **higher** of the short-term and
long-term rates.

4.4 Remaining life shall be calculated as:

    remaining_life (years) = (t_current - t_min) / governing_corrosion_rate

4.5 Where remaining life is less than 4 years, the vessel shall be placed on
the accelerated monitoring register and the inspection interval halved.

4.6 Where remaining life is less than 2 years, a repair, re-rating or
replacement plan shall be raised before the next operating campaign.

## 5. Findings Classification

| Severity | Definition | Required action | Approval authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| High | Immediate threat to containment; thickness below t-min; through-wall defect; failed relief device | Withdraw from service within 24 hours | Head of Inspection + Plant Manager |
| Medium | Remaining life below 4 years; active external corrosion; coating breakdown over 20% of surface; insulation damage permitting CUI | Repair within the current shutdown window | Head of Inspection |
| Low | Cosmetic corrosion; minor coating defects; labelling and access deficiencies | Rectify within 6 months | Inspection Engineer |

## 6. Approval Note Requirements

6.1 Every inspection resulting in a Medium or High severity finding shall be
accompanied by an approval note recommending the disposition of the vessel.

6.2 The approval note shall state, as a minimum:

- Vessel tag number and service description
- Date of inspection and inspection method employed
- Measured thickness readings against t-min
- Calculated corrosion rate and remaining life, showing the inputs used
- Severity classification per Clause 5
- Recommended disposition and the date by which it must be executed
- The clause of this SOP relied upon for each recommendation

6.3 An approval note is not valid until countersigned by the approving
authority named in Clause 5 for the highest severity finding recorded.

6.4 Any recommendation that a vessel continue in service with a Medium
finding shall record the compensating measures applied.

## 7. Records

All inspection records, thickness data sets, photographs and approval notes
shall be retained on the organisation's internal document management system
for the operating life of the vessel plus seven years. Records shall not be
transmitted outside the organisation's controlled network.
