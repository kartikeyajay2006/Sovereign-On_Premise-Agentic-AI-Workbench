## 1. Purpose

To define who may approve what, so that every decision affecting plant
integrity, expenditure or safety carries an identifiable and competent
approver.

## 2. Authority Matrix

Clauses 2.1 to 2.11 name, for each decision, who recommends it, who approves
it and who is notified. A role not named in a clause has no part in that
decision.

### 2.1 Accepting a Low Severity Finding

A Low severity finding is approved by the Inspection Engineer. No
recommendation is required, and the Head of Inspection, Plant Manager and
Director have no part in accepting a Low finding.

### 2.2 Accepting a Medium Severity Finding

A Medium severity finding is recommended by the Inspection Engineer and
approved by the Head of Inspection. The Plant Manager and the Director have
no part in accepting a Medium finding.

### 2.3 Accepting a High Severity Finding

A High severity finding is recommended by the Inspection Engineer and by the
Head of Inspection, and approved by the Plant Manager. The Director has no
part in accepting a High finding.

### 2.4 Withdrawing Equipment from Service

Withdrawal of equipment from service is initiated by the Inspection Engineer
and approved by the Head of Inspection. The Plant Manager is notified.

### 2.5 Returning Equipment to Service After Repair

Return of equipment to service after a repair is recommended by the
Inspection Engineer and approved by the Head of Inspection.

### 2.6 Re-Rating Equipment

Re-rating equipment to a reduced MAWP under SOP-INS-021 Clause 5 is
recommended by the Inspection Engineer and approved by both the Head of
Inspection and the Plant Manager. The Director is notified.

### 2.7 Extending an Inspection Interval

Extension of an inspection interval is recommended by the Inspection Engineer
and approved by the Head of Inspection. For a pressure vessel the extension
also needs the assessment required by SOP-INS-014 Clause 2.5.

### 2.8 Interim Operation Pending Assessment

Interim operation of equipment awaiting a Fitness-For-Service assessment,
under SOP-INS-021 Clause 6, is recommended by the Inspection Engineer and
approved by both the Head of Inspection and the Plant Manager.

### 2.9 Expenditure up to ₹5 Lakh

Expenditure, meaning the estimated installed cost of a decision, up to and
including ₹5 lakh is approved by the Head of Inspection. No recommendation is
required.

### 2.10 Expenditure Above ₹5 Lakh up to ₹50 Lakh

Expenditure, meaning the estimated installed cost of a decision, above
₹5 lakh and up to and including ₹50 lakh is recommended by the Head of
Inspection and approved by the Plant Manager.

### 2.11 Expenditure Above ₹50 Lakh

Expenditure, meaning the estimated installed cost of a decision, above
₹50 lakh is recommended by the Head of Inspection and by the Plant Manager,
and approved by the Director.

## 3. Rules of Application

### 3.1 Decisions Under More Than One Clause

Where a decision falls under more than one clause of Clause 2, the highest
authority named in any of those clauses applies.

### 3.2 No Self-Approval

An approver shall not approve a recommendation they themselves authored,
including one they authored jointly with others.

### 3.3 Delegation

Approval is personal and may not be delegated by email. Delegation during
absence requires a written delegation notice naming the delegate, the scope,
and the period, countersigned by the delegating authority.

### 3.4 Approval Without Evidence

An approval given without the evidence required by the governing procedure is
void, and the decision it recorded is treated as not taken.

### 3.5 Joint Signatures on a High Finding

For a High severity finding, SOP-INS-014 Clause 5.1 names the Head of
Inspection + Plant Manager: both sign the approval note, the Head of
Inspection recommending and the Plant Manager approving (Clause 2.3).

## 4. Approval Note Content

Every approval note shall carry at least:

- The decision being sought, stated in one sentence
- The equipment or subject affected, by tag number
- The findings relied upon, with their severity classification
- The calculations relied upon, with inputs shown
- The clause of the governing procedure authorising the decision
- The compensating measures, where the decision permits continued operation
- The recommending officer and approving authority, by name and role

## 5. Records

Approval notes are retained for the operating life of the equipment plus seven
years. An approval note that permits continued operation of degraded equipment
shall be reviewed at every subsequent inspection of that equipment.
