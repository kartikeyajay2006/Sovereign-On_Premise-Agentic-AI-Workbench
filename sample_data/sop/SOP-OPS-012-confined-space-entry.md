## 1. Purpose and Scope

This procedure controls entry into any confined space: a vessel, drum, column,
tank, pit or other enclosed space that has limited means of entry or exit and
is not designed for continuous occupancy. Internal inspection of a pressure
vessel is a confined space entry.

## 2. Roles

The Entry Supervisor authorises the entry and signs the entry permit. The
Attendant stays outside at the entry point throughout. Entrants are the
people who go in. The Authorised Gas Tester tests the atmosphere. One person
may not hold the Attendant role and the Entrant role at the same time.

## 3. Before Entry

### 3.1 Isolation for Entry

Every line connected to the space shall be positively isolated by blinding or
by disconnection and blanking under SOP-OPS-015 Clause 2.1. Closing valves,
even double block and bleed, is not isolation for entry.

### 3.2 Atmosphere Acceptance Limits

Entry shall not be authorised unless, at the time of the test, oxygen is
between 19.5% and 23.5% by volume, flammable gas is below 1% of the lower
explosive limit (LEL), hydrogen sulphide is below 1 ppm, and carbon monoxide
is below 25 ppm.

### 3.3 Gas Test Sequence

The atmosphere shall be tested not more than 30 minutes before entry, in the
order oxygen, then flammable gas, then toxic gases, at the top, middle and
bottom of the space.

## 4. During Entry

### 4.1 Attendant Duties

The Attendant keeps a log of every entrant, maintains continuous contact with
them, and summons rescue if needed. The Attendant shall never enter the space,
including to attempt a rescue.

### 4.2 Personal Gas Monitors and Evacuation

Every entrant wears a personal gas monitor. All entrants shall leave at once
if any monitor alarms, and entry shall not resume until the space has been
retested against Clause 3.2.

### 4.3 Permit Validity

An entry permit is valid for one shift of at most 12 hours. After any break in
entry longer than 60 minutes the atmosphere shall be retested before
re-entry.

## 5. Rescue

No entry shall start without a written rescue plan for that space. For a
vertical entry deeper than 1.5 m, a tripod, full-body harness and retrieval
line shall be rigged at the entry point, and a rescue team with breathing
apparatus shall be on standby.

## 6. Hot Work in a Confined Space

Hot work inside a confined space needs a hot work permit under SOP-OPS-011 as
well as the entry permit, and both permits shall be displayed at the entry
point (SOP-OPS-011 Clause 5.3).

## 7. Incidents

An evacuation on alarm, an injury, or a breach of any permit condition during
an entry shall be reported under SOP-HSE-004 Clause 2.
